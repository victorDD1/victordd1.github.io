## About Me

I'm a PhD candidate at the [Technical University of Munich (TUM)](https://www.tum.de/en/), advised by Prof. Majid Khadiv, with a background in machine learning and robotics. My research focuses on **solving loco-manipulation tasks with a humanoid robot**.

Before that, I received my Master's degree in Data and Information Sciences from [CentraleSupélec](https://www.centralesupelec.fr/en), and spent time at the [Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems](https://is.mpg.de/), the [French Atomic Energy Commission (CEA)](https://www.cea.fr/english) and [Thales](https://www.thalesgroup.com/en).

<p><b><i style="color: #043361!important;">I'm exploring a potential transition from academia to industry</i></b>. Feel free to reach out.</p>

## Research Interests

- **Humanoid Loco-Manipulation**: whole-body skills in constrained environments.
- **Synthetic Data Generation**: dynamically-feasible motion retargeting and trajectory optimization.
- **Contact Planning**: learning-based and sampling-based planning for dynamic legged locomotion.
